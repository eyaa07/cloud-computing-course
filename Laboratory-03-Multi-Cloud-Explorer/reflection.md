# Mission 3 Reflection

## 1. Which cloud platform impressed you the most?

The cloud platform that impressed me the most is Google Cloud Platform. I liked it because it has many useful services for Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, data, and Kubernetes. I also learned that Google Cloud can be used to run applications and store data. Before this activity, I only knew a little about cloud platforms, so learning about Google Cloud helped me understand what it can do.

## 2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?

AWS, Azure, and GCP are all cloud computing platforms. They all provide services for computing, storage, databases, networking, and other cloud needs. They also allow users to use cloud resources without needing to manage all the physical hardware themselves. Even though they have different services and names, they can be used to solve many of the same problems.

## 3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?

Business requirements are important when choosing a cloud platform because every company has different needs. For example, a company that uses many Microsoft products may choose Azure, while a company focused on AI may choose Google Cloud. A startup may also choose a platform that can grow with the company. The best cloud platform is the one that matches the company's needs and goals.

## 4. What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?

I learned that choosing a cloud platform is not only about choosing the most popular one. I need to look at what the company needs before making a decision. I also learned how to compare cloud services from AWS, Azure, and GCP. This activity helped me understand how cloud services can be used to solve real business problems.
